About Lesotho

A mountain kingdom surrounded by South Africa.

Welcome to the Kingdom in the Sky

Lesotho is a mountainous country in Southern Africa. Its capital city is Maseru. The country is known for its high altitude, scenic landscapes and rich Basotho heritage.

Visitors can discover traditional villages, mountain passes, nature reserves and local crafts. The Sesotho language and the Basotho blanket are important parts of the country's identity.

Why visit?

  • Beautiful mountain scenery.
  • Unique Basotho culture and history.
  • Outdoor activities and peaceful rural areas.
  • Warm hospitality from local communities.

Travel responsibly

Respect local customs, ask permission before photographing people, support local businesses and leave natural areas clean.
